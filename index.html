A browser cannot count as fast as a fast link delivers. For anything above a few gigabits, or on a machine without a browser:

curl -o /dev/null http://THIS-SERVER/10G
wget -O /dev/null http://THIS-SERVER/10G

The files themselves, nothing but zeros: 100M1G10G100G

This measures the way between you and this server, not your internet connection. Sizes are binary: 1G is 1024 MB.
